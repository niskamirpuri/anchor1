FAQ

Questions you might have.

Do I need any experience to apply?
Not at all. Anchor is specifically designed for students who have never had a job. Every business knows they\'re welcoming a learner — that's the whole point.
How long are the placements?
Placements range from 5 to 20 hours per week. Duration varies by business but is always short, focused, and manageable alongside school.
Will my parents need to be involved?
Yes, and that's a good thing. We keep parents informed throughout the process and provide all the information they need to feel confident.
What if I don\'t enjoy the placement?
That's actually useful information! Discovering what you don\'t enjoy is just as valuable as finding what you love. Reach out to us anytime if you need support.
Are there remote options?
Yes. Many placements are available remotely or as hybrid. You can filter by type when browsing opportunities.
Is there a cost to join Anchor?
Anchor is completely free for students. There is no fee to register, apply, or participate in any placement.

The Process

Simple from start to finish.

We've designed the business experience to be as light-touch as possible — so you can focus on what you do best.

🌱 Step 03

Trust & Safety

How we verify partner businesses.

Every business on Anchor goes through a structured review before students can apply. We take our duty of care to students seriously.

🏢

Business Identity Check

We verify legal business registration details before any listing goes live. Only registered entities are permitted on the platform.

📋

Safeguarding Agreement

All businesses must sign Anchor's safeguarding commitment, agreeing to child-safe practices and a designated point of contact for every student.

📞

Direct Vetting Call

Before approval, a member of the Anchor team holds a short onboarding call to confirm the placement is genuine, structured, and appropriate.

✅

Approved Badge

Verified businesses receive an Anchor Approved status visible to students and parents. Listings remain active only while businesses remain in good standing.

🔄

Ongoing Review

After each placement, Anchor collects feedback from both student and business. Any concerns are investigated and can result in suspension from the platform.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parental Consent Required

Every student application requires verified parental consent before it reaches a business. Parents are kept informed throughout the process.
